I’ve watched [Cindy’s YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@readwithcindy) for years. I've enjoyed her book review with her quick-witted remarks and open honesty about the books she has read. Her reviews are not only entertaining for half a million subscribers on her YouTube channel, but informative and well-thought-out. She’s one of the best BookTubers to follow.

Now Cindy has written her debut, The Secret World of Briar Rose, and let me tell you –

Cindy Pham is a talented author.

Her debut is a retelling of the movie The Sleeping Beauty, in which the kingdom of Gyldan has been doomed since the princess Amelia was cursed over a hundred years ago. Corin, an orphan thief, is looking for her younger sister, Elly, when Corin falls into Amelia’s dream world. There, Corin sees the beautiful, fantastical world that Amelia’s alter ego, Briar Rose, holds. But good things do not last forever, and eventually Amelia’s nightmares are bound to come true.

This is a beautiful story that twists the idea of the curse into a gift for Amelia, as life as a princess is not as easy as it sounds. For Amelia, being stuck in a world where she can change the scenery at a moment's notice sounds like a dream (pun intended), but for Corin, she’s waiting for the other shoe to drop, as she has since her parents passed away. Corin cannot enjoy the cozy cottage in Springland or the bubbling waves in Summerland because of how grief-stricken she is over the real world. Corin considers herself a realist whose only goal is to keep Corin and her sister safe.

There's no room for Corin to paint or support anyone’s cause because nothing short of survival will keep a roof over their heads. It’s Corin’s biggest struggle to believe there’s more to life than surviving when life seems to be all about war and destruction. This is what makes Corin the most well-developed character in the book because of how the author shows Corin’s struggle with living versus surviving, and how being told to “stop and smell the roses” isn’t enough.
In every other chapter, we see the events leading up to the day Amelia is cursed, and why she prefers escapism, while we see what motivates Corin to leave the dreamworld with her sister, Elly. I can honestly say that both reveals are gut-wrenching and powerful to the story.

Another character I loved is Malicine. Following their journey from bitter demon to Amelia’s best friend was my favorite part. Since birth, they were severely punished by their sisters for looking neither human nor fairy, but like a monster. Reading Malicine’s point of view shows that villains are made, not born. Malicine can be a powerful ally or a dangerous enemy. As Malicine’s and Amelia’s worlds collide, the untold history between the fairies and humans is brought to light.

The Secret World of Briar Rose is a powerful story about grief, escapism, and endless dreams. It’ll remind you that you are strong enough to face the real world and that there are people who love you enough to help you through it. That’s why this is another five-star read for me.
